# LC 2474. Customers With Strictly Increasing Purchases

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/customers-with-strictly-increasing-purchases/) · Premium · Tags: Database

## 题意

找出所有月份消费都严格递增且月份连续的顾客。

## 最优思路

按月聚合后 LAG 检查相邻月连续且金额严格增大。

## 图解

```text
SQL 窗口/聚合/透视 → 结果
```
