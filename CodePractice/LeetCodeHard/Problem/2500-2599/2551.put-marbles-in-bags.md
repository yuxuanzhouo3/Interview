# LC 2551. Put Marbles in Bags

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/put-marbles-in-bags/) · Tags: Array, Greedy, Sorting

## 题意

把数组切成 `k` 段；得分 = 每段两端之和的总和；求最大得分与最小得分之差。

## 最优思路

固定首尾 `weights[0]+weights[n-1]`；切点贡献 `weights[i]+weights[i+1]`。选 `k-1` 个最大/最小邻对和作差。

## 图解

```text
score = w[0]+w[n-1] + Σ cut-pairs
diff = Σ top(k-1) − Σ bottom(k-1)
```

## 复杂度

| 项 | 值 |
|----|----|
| 时间 | \(O(n\log n)\) |
| 空间 | \(O(n)\) |
