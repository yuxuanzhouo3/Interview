# LC 3203. Find Minimum Diameter After Merging Two Trees

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/find-minimum-diameter-after-merging-two-trees/) · Tags: Tree, BFS, Graph

## 题意

两棵树各选一点连一条边合并，使新树直径最小。

## 最优思路

新直径 = `max(d1, d2, ceil(d1/2)+ceil(d2/2)+1)`。分别求两树直径即可。

## 图解

```text
connect centers (approx radii)
ans = max(d1,d2,r1+r2+1)
```
