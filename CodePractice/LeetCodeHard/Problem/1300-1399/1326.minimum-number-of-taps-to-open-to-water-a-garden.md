# LC 1326. Minimum Number of Taps to Open to Water a Garden

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-number-of-taps-to-open-to-water-a-garden/) · Tags: Array, Greedy, Dynamic Programming

## 题意

`[0,n]` 花园，位置 `i` 水龙头覆盖 `[i-ranges[i], i+ranges[i]]`，求最少开几个水龙头。

## 最优思路

转化成「从左端点能跳到的最远右端」跳跃游戏贪心。

## 图解

```text
left → max_right[left]  like jump game II
```
