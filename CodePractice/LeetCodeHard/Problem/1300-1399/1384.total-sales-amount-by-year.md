# LC 1384. Total Sales Amount by Year

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/total-sales-amount-by-year/) · Premium · Tags: Database

## 题意

把跨年销售按 2018–2020 拆成年销售额。

## 最优思路

与年份维表相交，天数 = DATEDIFF(LEAST(end), GREATEST(start))+1。
