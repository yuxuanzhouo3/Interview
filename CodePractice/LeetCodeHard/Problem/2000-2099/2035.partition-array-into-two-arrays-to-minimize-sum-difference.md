# LC 2035. Partition Array Into Two Arrays to Minimize Sum Difference

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/partition-array-into-two-arrays-to-minimize-sum-difference/) · Tags: Array, Two Pointers, Binary Search, Dynamic Programming, Bit Manipulation, Meet in the Middle, Sorting, Ordered Set, Bitmask

## 题意

`2n` 个数均分两组，使两组和之差绝对值最小。

## 最优思路

折半枚举左/右各取若干个数的子集和，对每组个数 `k` 二分凑近 `total/2`。

## 复杂度

| 项 | 值 |
|----|----|
| 时间 | \(O(2^{n/2} \cdot n)\) |
| 空间 | \(O(2^{n/2})\) |
