# LC 185. Department Top Three Salaries

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/department-top-three-salaries/)

## 题意

查每个部门工资前三高的员工（并列同薪同名次）。

## 最优思路

窗口函数 `DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY departmentId ORDER BY salary DESC)`，取 `rk <= 3`。

## 图解

```text
Dept A salaries: 100,90,90,80 → ranks 1,2,2,3 → keep all four rows with rk<=3
```
