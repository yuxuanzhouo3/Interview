# LC 801. Minimum Swaps To Make Sequences Increasing

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-swaps-to-make-sequences-increasing/)

## 题意

两数组同下标交换最少次使都严格递增。

## 最优思路

`keep/swap` 两状态 DP。

## 图解

```text
keep[i]/swap[i] transitions
```

## 复杂度

| 项 | 值 |
|----|----|
| 时间 | \(O(n)\) |
| 空间 | \(O(1)\) |
