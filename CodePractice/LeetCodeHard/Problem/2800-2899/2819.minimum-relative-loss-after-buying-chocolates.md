# LC 2819. Minimum Relative Loss After Buying Chocolates

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-relative-loss-after-buying-chocolates/) · Tags: Array, Binary Search, Sorting, Prefix Sum

## 题意

对每个询问 `(k, m)`：买恰好 `m` 块巧克力，相对损失为 Bob 付款减去 Alice 付款；`p≤k` 时损失为 `p`，否则为 `2k-p`。求最小损失。

## 最优思路

排序+前缀和。便宜侧取最小价，昂贵侧取最高价；二分左侧取值个数使边际损失最优。

## 图解

```text
sorted: [cheap ≤k | expensive >k]
         ← take left     take right →
loss: p              2k-p
```
