# LC 2835. Minimum Operations to Form Subsequence With Target Sum

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-operations-to-form-subsequence-with-target-sum/) · Tags: Array, Greedy, Bit Manipulation

## 题意

`nums` 均为 2 的幂；可把 `2^x` 拆成两个 `2^{x-1}`；求组成和为 `target` 的子序列最少拆分次数。

## 最优思路

按位计数；缺位则向高位借并拆分计数；低位多余可合并进高位。

## 图解

```text
need bit i → if none, split from higher bit (cost = distance)
```

## 复杂度

| 项 | 值 |
|----|----|
| 时间 | \(O(n+32^2)\) |
| 空间 | \(O(1)\) |
