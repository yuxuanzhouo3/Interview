# LC 2813. Maximum Elegance of a K-Length Subsequence

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-elegance-of-a-k-length-subsequence/) · Tags: Greedy, Sorting

## 题意

选 `k` 个物品；优雅度 = 利润和 + 不同类别数²；求最大优雅度。

## 最优思路

按利润降序取前 `k`；之后用更高多样性替换「重复类别中利润最小者」。

## 图解

```text
top-k by profit → stack of duplicate profits
later new cat replaces dup.pop()
```
