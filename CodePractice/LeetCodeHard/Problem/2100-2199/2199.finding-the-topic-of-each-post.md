# LC 2199. Finding the Topic of Each Post

> Hard · [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/finding-the-topic-of-each-post/) · Premium · Tags: Database

## 题意

按关键词匹配帖子主题；无匹配输出 Ambiguous!。

## 最优思路

词边界 LIKE 连接 Keywords，GROUP_CONCAT DISTINCT topic_id。

## 图解

```text
content ∋ word → topic_id
```
